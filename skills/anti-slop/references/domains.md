# Adapt the contract to the domain

This routing layer is editorial synthesis, not measured prevalence or five mandatory templates. Evidence anchors: S02/S09 for scholarly claims, S13 for documentation, S08/S10 for rhetoric, S15 for workplace reader cost. No slide-specific controlled study was established in this review.

## Scientific and technical prose

Protect methods, samples, comparator, units, definitions of uncertainty, negative findings and limits. Do not replace 'associated with' by 'caused', 'standard deviation' by 'confidence interval', or percentage points by percent. Specificity matters where a result must be interpreted, not in every sentence by quota. Keep repeated technical terminology and necessary passive constructions. Check each citation's identity and exact support. A fluent summary cannot replace reading the underlying evidence.

## Documentation

Name what the system does, prerequisites, version boundaries, defaults, errors and recovery steps. Preserve commands, flags, paths and identifiers byte-for-byte unless the task explicitly changes them. Distinguish attempts from retries. Repeat the same term for the same object; synonym variety is often harmful. Numbered steps and bold warnings can be useful. Never invent behavior to replace a promotional adjective. First-party Google guidance S13 explicitly values consistency and helper words for global audiences; that is documentation guidance, not a rule for all prose.

## Blogs and social posts

State the particular thought rather than promising a revolution. Preserve actual opinions, jokes, memorable phrasing and deliberate rhetorical moves. A post need not contain a personal anecdote, contrarian claim or takeaway. First-person experience must come from the author, not be fabricated by the model. Disclose uncertainty and source limits without smothering the point. Do not force every post into hook/list/moral/call-to-action.

## Slides and speeches

Distinguish the projected slide, a circulated deck and the spoken script. A slide can be concise while notes carry detail, but visible qualifications needed to interpret the claim must remain visible, especially when the slide travels alone. Repetition, parallelism and fragments can help listeners; a report's sentence rhythm is not automatically an orator's. Do not turn preliminary findings into a definitive headline. Preserve data provenance, dates and comparison basis.

## Everyday correspondence

Make the ask, relevant context, owner and deadline discoverable. Preserve time zones, attachment names, default actions, consent and degrees of commitment. Silence is not approval unless that default was explicitly established. Politeness and hedges can protect relationships; do not delete them simply because they are conventional. Avoid compulsory executive-summary formatting for a two-line message, but retain structure when a complex coordination task needs it.

## Language and voice

Follow the requested target language rather than merely mirroring the prompt's language. Preserve names, identifiers and quotations as needed. English rhetoric examples are not universal detection features. Cross-language studies S03/S14 establish reasons to inspect transfer, not an all-language recipe. Ask for local editorial judgment when you cannot assess the intended register. No special language is privileged by this package.
