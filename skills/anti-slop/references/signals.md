# Signals are questions, not bans

These are editorial diagnostics, not statistically validated classifiers. Sources and their evidence types are in [evidence.md](evidence.md), particularly S02, S04, S05, S08–S10. Frequency and context matter; no numerical threshold is prescribed.

| Signal | Inspect the actual defect | Keep when |
|---|---|---|
| 'Not just X, but Y'; 'No A. No B. Just C.' | Is X a real misconception or merely a straw premise invented to make Y dramatic? | The distinction is true, relevant and worth emphasizing. |
| Dashes in nearly every sentence | Is the aside useful, or does punctuation conceal an unclear relation? | The interruption adds precision, rhythm or the author's voice. |
| Bold labels, headings, tables and bullets everywhere | Is structure replacing explanation or breaking a continuous thought? | Scanning, navigation, comparison or procedural order needs it. |
| Uniform short fragments | Were 'because', 'although', 'if' or 'therefore' lost with the logical relation? | Urgency, dialogue, a punchline or projected slides calls for that rhythm. |
| Triads and parallel phrases | Does each member add something distinct? | There really are three distinct points, or deliberate repetition helps listeners. |
| 'Pivotal', 'transformative', 'robust', 'seamless' | What supports this evaluation? What changed and under which conditions? | The claim is substantiated or clearly presented as the author's opinion. |
| 'Delve', 'tapestry', 'landscape', 'unlock' | Is the word exact in this sentence, or an interchangeable flourish? | It names the intended action/image. Never replace a technical term just because it is common. |
| 'Highlighting the importance of...' tails | Does the added clause follow from the evidence, or merely restate significance? | It identifies a supported consequence. |
| 'Studies show', 'experts agree' | Which sources support this exact proposition? Is disagreement being erased? | Nearby attribution and scope justify the wording. |
| Real-looking citations | Does the cited passage entail the attached claim? Are quotation, date, author and source genuine? | Citation identity and claim alignment have actually been checked. |
| Repeated implications without a claim | What insight is the text approaching but never stating? | Deliberate suspense or privacy constraints explain the omission; do not expose protected information for specificity. |
| Symmetrical pros/cons and generic 'balance' | Are the alternatives genuinely relevant and comparably supported? | A neutral comparison is the reader's task. Do not force an opinion into a reference entry. |
| Hedging in every clause | What precisely is uncertain? Is this caution useful, tactful or required? | The qualifier protects accuracy, scope, consent or the relationship. |
| Confident summary or moral at every ending | Does this ending add a warranted conclusion, action or memorable point? | The genre needs closure and the material supports it. |
| Metaphor explained past usefulness | Which shared property clarifies the subject? Does the analogy smuggle a false mechanism? | The image works, or deliberate incongruity is part of the art. |
| Generic niceness and enthusiastic validation | Is the tone appropriate, or does it flatter instead of answering? | Warmth and politeness serve the relationship. Do not replace them with brusqueness by default. |

## Overcorrection to avoid

Replacing 'delve' with 'explore' repairs no missing argument. Turning every sentence into a fragment loses logical relations. Adding invented measurements or personal memories creates false substance. Removing uncertainty can turn correlation into causation. Varying the name of one API component creates ambiguity. Stripping every dash or heading makes an anti-slop house style rather than better writing.

When specificity is missing, use what is known, ask for the missing fact, or mark a placeholder. Do not fill it with a plausible number. A plain falsehood is still a falsehood.
